# Freeborn privacy policy

In effect from October 14, 2026.

## The short version

Freeborn keeps what you type on your phone so it can show you your plan, and sends it nowhere. It has no accounts, never connects to your bank, shows no ads and does not track you.

## What Freeborn keeps

What you enter: each debt (its name, type, balance, interest rate, minimum payment, due day and whether it is on autopay), the payments and gifts you log, the choices that shape your plan, for each debt when you last told it a payment had not gone through yet, the lessons you have finished, which milestones the app has marked with you, and anything you write in a reflection.

It also keeps a note that you have begun, and your reminder settings.

## Where it is kept

On your phone, in Freeborn’s own storage. Freeborn does not send it anywhere: there is no Freeborn server and no account, and nobody at Freeborn can see what you enter.

If your phone backs itself up (to iCloud or a computer on an iPhone, or to Google on Android), what Freeborn keeps is part of that backup, on the terms of whoever provides it. That backup is what brings your entries to a new phone. With backups turned off, a lost or erased phone takes your entries with it.

## Reminders

The morning verse and payment reminders are scheduled on your phone. No server sends them, and Freeborn sends none unless you turn them on. You can turn them off in Settings at any time.

A payment reminder says that a payment is due, never which debt, so a locked phone does not show the names of your debts.

## Sharing

When you share your debt-free date, the picture is made on your phone and goes only where you send it. It leaves out your dollar amounts unless you choose to include them.

When you export your data, the files are made on your phone and go only where you send them.

On Android, the share sheet keeps its own copy of what you send until it is next used. Freeborn removes that copy the next time it opens, and when you erase everything.

## What Freeborn does not do

It does not ask for your bank login or connect to any financial account. It does not show ads. It includes no advertising, analytics or tracking software. It collects nothing, so there is nothing for it to sell or share. It does not read your contacts, location or photos.

## Your choices

You can save a copy of your debts, payments, gifts and reflections: Settings, then Export your data.

You can erase everything: Settings, then Erase everything. This removes your debts, payments, gifts, reflections and your place on the path from your phone. It cannot be undone. Your reminder settings stay until you delete the app, and deleting the app removes everything it keeps.

A copy already in a phone backup stays there until that backup is replaced or deleted. That is between you and whoever provides the backup.

Freeborn’s author holds no copy of your information, so there is none to show you, correct or delete on request. Write to the address below with any question about this policy.

## Children

Freeborn is not meant for children under 13, and collects no information from anyone.
